Why Buenos Aires?

Go here if you want the deepest pool of experienced developers and data engineers.

  • Around 85% of Argentinian developers are based here.
  • Strong ecosystem: startups, scale-ups and established tech companies.
  • Good English proficiency and near-shore time zone for US/EU teams.
  • Excellent remote-work infrastructure and connectivity.
  • Favourable cost–quality ratio for engineering talent.

Best use cases

  • Senior data engineers and technical leads.
  • People coordinating international / distributed teams.
  • Situations where slightly higher salary expectations are acceptable.
💡 Think of Buenos Aires as your "default hub" when quality and depth matter most.

Why Córdoba?

  • Described as the country's second-largest tech hub.
  • Strong academic base: engineering & IT graduates continuously fueling talent.
  • Responsible for 30–40% of Argentine software exports.
  • Similar time zone, but lower cost than the capital.

Best use cases

  • Very good mid–senior engineers at lower rates.
  • Long-term near-shore teams where cost vs quality matters.
💡 Think of Córdoba as your "smart value" choice for strong, export-oriented talent.

🎓 Education

Education context & top universities

Understand how Argentina's higher education system works and which universities matter most for engineering, software, and data roles.

🏛️ System structure
  • Programs typically last 6 years, with early specialisation.
  • Public universities are tuition-free and highly prestigious.
  • Private institutions charge lower fees vs. global peers.
  • Admission often open; evaluated through entry exams.
🎒 Student experience
  • Most students live at home and commute to class.
  • Combining work and study is very common.
  • Education model is urban and independent.
🏆 Top Technical Universities
  • University of Buenos Aires (UBA) – Faculty of Engineering / FCEyN
  • Universidad Tecnológica Nacional (UTN) – Buenos Aires Campus
  • Instituto Tecnológico de Buenos Aires (ITBA)
  • Universidad Torcuato Di Tella (UTDT)
  • Universidad Nacional de Tres de Febrero (UNTREF)
💰 Compensation

Employment & compensation framework

How full-time contracts, contractor setups and macroeconomic context shape compensation for technical talent in Argentina.

📄 Core characteristics
  • Employees engaged under formal, full-time contracts.
  • Includes labor protections, health insurance and retirement contributions.
  • Payroll automatically applies all statutory deductions.
🎁 Bonus & deductions
  • Includes 13th-month bonus ("Aguinaldo") paid in June and December.
  • Typical deductions range between 25–35%.
  • Covers health insurance, retirement, income tax and Law 19032.
🔄 Market practices
  • Contractors face fewer deductions and often earn higher net pay.
  • Flexibility to invoice in foreign currencies.
  • Common in startups and export-oriented companies.
✨ Typical benefits
  • Unlimited PTO and private healthcare often included.
  • Stock options or performance bonuses in senior roles.
🎯 Talent segment
  • Senior engineers prefer contractor structures.
  • They benchmark against remote/near-shore markets.
  • Salary data often underestimates their real expectations.
📊 Interpreting data
  • Public salary reports exclude contracting rates.
  • Real expectations higher due to USD-linked payments.
  • Strong English and international experience add premium value.
📈 Key indicators
  • Argentina has high inflation in ARS and USD.
  • New taxable salary components since 2024.
  • Increasing deductions for registered employees.
💼 Impact on offers
  • Talent prioritises net income and contract type.
  • Contractor roles mitigate inflation impact.
  • Offers must account for currency exposure and volatility.
🎯 Strategy

Market context & hiring strategies

Overview of Argentina's tech hiring landscape and effective strategies to attract and retain senior technical talent.

🌎 Market context
  • Argentina has a strong pool of skilled developers with good English and cultural alignment.
  • Demand is very high — competition for senior talent is intense.
  • Few inbound applicants — most hires come from proactive sourcing and referrals.
📣 Employer brand & sourcing
  • Employer brand: Highlight product-company work, growth, stability and transparency.
  • Targeted sourcing: LinkedIn, local networks, referrals and university ties.
🤝 Process & retention
  • Clear, competitive offers: USD-based pay, inflation adjustments, non-monetary benefits.
  • Efficient process: Concise technical interviews; timely feedback matters.
  • Retention focus: Mentorship, career paths and regular check-ins.
🤝 Culture

Cultural context

Work style, communication norms and expectations when collaborating with Argentine teams.

💬 Communication style
  • Argentine professionals avoid confrontation — proactive clarification reduces misalignment.
  • Informal, personable tone builds trust; warmth and conversation go a long way.
  • Top developers are often employed and reluctant to engage in long interview processes.
⚖️ Work & career priorities
  • Work-life balance, autonomy and flexibility are top priorities, even for senior talent.
  • The market is agency-driven; some lack experience in product-focused environments.
  • Product-company positioning resonates strongly with top engineers.
